# Product thesis

## Category

**Decision observability** is the continuous instrumentation of how an organization forms, commits to, and learns from consequential decisions.

Traditional analytics observes the business. Decision intelligence recommends an action. FAULTLINE observes the decision system itself.

## Ideal customer

The initial wedge is a 200–2,000 person AI, fintech, infrastructure, or regulated-software company where strategic calls cross product, revenue, security, legal, and operations. These teams already have data and AI tools; their failure mode is contradictory local truth.

## Core job

> Before we commit millions of dollars or months of work, show us which assumption is fragile, which evidence is missing, and what would make us change our mind.

## Product loop

1. Ingest decisions, plans, evidence, and outcome metrics.
2. Normalize claims into a temporal belief graph.
3. Detect contradiction, missing evidence, and assumption drift.
4. Rank faults by impact and decision deadline.
5. Retrieve the evidence chain and run counterfactuals.
6. Seal the pre-commit belief state.
7. Resolve outcomes and score calibration.
8. Improve future priors and organizational judgment.

## North-star metric

**Avoided decision regret**: weighted economic exposure reduced by a pre-commit intervention that changed the option, timing, scope, or evidence plan.

Supporting metrics include time-to-decision, evidence coverage, forecast Brier score, contradiction half-life, and percentage of major decisions with explicit reversal gates.

## Why now

Generative AI makes plausible recommendations abundant. It also makes it easier for every function to produce polished but mutually inconsistent analyses. As the cost of generating a position approaches zero, the scarce asset becomes governed organizational judgment.
